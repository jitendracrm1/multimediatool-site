Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Cancellation

There is no subscription to cancel. Every pass is a one-time payment for a fixed period and never renews automatically. You may stop using the service at any time.

Refunds

You may ask for a full refund within 2 days of buying a pass.

  • Duplicate or accidental charge: if you were charged more than once for the same pass, we refund the extra payment.
  • Payment taken but pass not activated: if your money was debited and your pass did not start, we activate it or refund you.
  • A fault we cannot fix: if a tool included in your pass does not work and we cannot fix it, we refund the unused part of your pass.

How to ask

Email jitendra@neuravix.ai from your account email, with the date of purchase and the payment reference shown in your Razorpay receipt. We reply within 2 working days.

How long it takes

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank or card issuer may need extra time to show it.
